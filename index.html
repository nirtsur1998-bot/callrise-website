A desktop assistant for sales calls. It listens with you, coaches you live, and handles the follow-up.

Live transcription & coaching

See what was said as it happens, with prompts to help you handle objections and next steps.

Meeting prep

Connect your Google or Outlook calendar and get a short brief before each meeting, built from your own notes.

Summaries & tasks

Your calendar, your choice

Connecting a calendar is optional. CallRise AI first asks for read-only access, which lets it show your meetings and prepare briefs. It asks for permission to edit events only if you turn on two-way sync. You can disconnect at any time in Settings. How we handle calendar data is set out in our Privacy Policy.
